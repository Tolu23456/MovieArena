Phase 8 draft v0.1

Motion that explains, not decorates.

The product has 0, 120, 160, and 240ms. Nothing moves to show off: cards never lift, art never zooms, nothing glows. Toggle reduced motion at any time — the page keeps every function and drops translation, fade, shimmer, and rotation. Controls respond instantly; the focus ring never animates. The toggle simulates the OS preference for inspection; production follows the OS setting.
